# Captain Campaign

```text
Create Captain Campaign, an original satirical civic-pageantry fighter. Shape language: wide triangular podium silhouette, oversized foam hands, uneven papier-mache smile, generic starburst pins with no political colors/symbols, and a collapsing microphone-sceptre. Palette: brick red, parchment cream, faded teal, and brass. Do not reference any real politician, slogan, hairstyle, suit, flag, or campaign.

Combat identity: mid-range space control. Normals use foam-hand swats and podium panels; specials deploy cardboard barricades, a harmless ticker-tape gust, and a microphone lunge; super calls a generic parade float. Make startup/recovery readable through squash/stretch and held stop-motion poses.

Produce: neutral turnaround; 8-12 frame idle; forward/back walk; dash; crouch; jump rise/apex/fall/land; block high/low; six normals; three command normals; three specials; throw; hit reactions; knockdown/wakeup; super; taunt; intro; win/lose; and finisher poses. Include collision boxes and event frame notes per move.

Finisher: “Approval Rating Avalanche.” Gameplay freezes after a valid KO command. Shot 1: close view of a wobbling podium. Shot 2: Captain Campaign waves the sceptre and harmless paper ballots swirl in. Shot 3: a giant papier-mache wave covers the rival. Shot 4: the wave opens to reveal the rival dusty but intact, giving a confused thumbs-up. Return to results pose. No real slogans or symbols.
```
