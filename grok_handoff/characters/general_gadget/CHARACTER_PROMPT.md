# General Gadget

```text
Create General Gadget, an original satire of tech solutionism. Shape language: angular tinkerer jumpsuit, modular cardboard-and-clay backpack, generic mini drones, mismatched safety visor, and a portable charging brick. Palette: graphite, orange, cyan, and recycled cardboard brown. Do not use a real founder’s name, likeness, product name, vehicle shape, company logo, or speech style.

Combat identity: agile movement, drone-assisted zoning, and experimental moves with comic failure risk. Normals use folded tool arms. Specials launch a tiny drone, hover briefly with fan effects, and deploy a wobbling delivery bot. The super is a comically oversized generic “prototype shell,” not a recognizable powered suit. Produce full shared animation/move/box/event deliverables.

Finisher: “Autonomous Delivery.” Shot 1: drones project a generic delivery route line. Shot 2: a tiny cardboard autonomous cart rolls in and loses a wheel. Shot 3: it gently bumps the rival into a mound of foam packing peanuts and cardboard panels. Shot 4: the rival emerges wearing a box as a hat while General Gadget takes an awkward bow. No vehicle-brand references.
```
