# General Gravy

```text
Create General Gravy, an original satire of theatrical cooking spectacle. Shape language: round clay cook with a medal-covered apron, a chef hat shaped like a folded recipe card, oversized ceramic ladle, and stove-mitt gauntlets. Palette: tomato red, oven black, cream, and copper. Do not use a real chef’s face, accent, phrases, restaurant identity, or clothing.

Combat identity: heavy swings, short heat zones, and slow armor. Normals use ladle arcs and rolling-pin pokes. Specials create harmless flour clouds, a bouncing dough ball, and a sizzling skillet floor marker. Build the full animation/move inventory, readable anticipation, and authored box/event sheets.

Finisher: “Recipe Revision.” Shot 1: General Gravy reveals a giant blank recipe card. Shot 2: a flour poof turns the rival into a harmless oversized clay dumpling. Shot 3: the ladle rings a dinner bell; confetti herbs fall. Shot 4: the dumpling unfolds into the rival, wobbly and embarrassed. Return safely to match results.
```
