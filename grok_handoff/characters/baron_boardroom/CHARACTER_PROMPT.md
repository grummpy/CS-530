# Baron Boardroom

```text
Create Baron Boardroom, an original satire of corporate excess. Shape language: broad rectangular padded pinstripe suit, absurdly long snake-like tie, square gold briefcase gauntlets, and tiny reading glasses with no celebrity resemblance. Palette: charcoal, dull gold, invoice white, and green ledger accents. Avoid real brands, executive likenesses, or finance-company symbols.

Combat identity: slow armored pressure with a risk/reward “quarterly meter.” Normals are briefcase swings, tie flicks, and shoulder checks. Specials are receipt paper traps, a rolling office chair, and a coin-shaped clay projectile. Provide the complete shared animation/move inventory and move-frame documentation.

Finisher: “Quarterly Reckoning.” Shot 1: Baron opens an oversized blank invoice. Shot 2: cartoon paper hands stamp “PAID” symbols that are abstract and unbranded. Shot 3: folding cardboard forms a shipping crate around the rival. Shot 4: crate opens into harmless confetti and the rival is seated on it, dizzy but intact. Restore camera/HUD/state.
```
