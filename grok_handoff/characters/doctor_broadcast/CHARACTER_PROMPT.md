# Doctor Broadcast

```text
Create Doctor Broadcast, an original satire of attention-driven wellness media. Shape language: tall narrow clay host, abstract crystal helmet, yoga-mat cape, rotating ring-light halo, and many fictional product badges. Palette: lavender, seafoam, white, and neon coral. Do not imitate a real host, podcaster, logo, medicine brand, or voice.

Combat identity: traps, counters, and misleading visual feints. Normals use foam paddles and ring-light spins. Specials place soft spotlight zones, toss harmless foam “supplement” bottles, and use a meditation counter. Build a complete shared animation/move inventory, box data, and VFX event list.

Finisher: “Sponsored Break.” Shot 1: the ring light expands into a generic studio frame. Shot 2: the rival becomes a flat cardboard product-cutout with a silly barcode. Shot 3: Doctor Broadcast points to floating fake-review stars. Shot 4: the cutout unfolds and rival steps out covered in stickers. All labels are invented and unbranded.
```
