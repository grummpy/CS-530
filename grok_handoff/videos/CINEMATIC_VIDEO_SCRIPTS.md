# Cinematic Finisher Video Prompt Sheets

```text
Create short, skippable, 4-6 second in-engine cinematic sequences, not pre-rendered videos. They are data-driven timelines controlling simulation lock, fighter pose/trajectory, camera shot, animation event, VFX, audio, subtitle, and cleanup. Each sequence begins only from the finisher window after a valid KO command, commits the round result deterministically, restores camera/HUD/input state, and is safe to skip in training. Use no realistic injury, blood, sexual framing, real names, brands, political symbols, or imitation dialogue.

Produce scripts/storyboards for these six finishers:

1. Captain Campaign — Approval Rating Avalanche
00:00-00:01: podium close-up; Captain says, “Let the paper parade begin!”
00:01-00:03: papier-mache wave and blank ballots swirl in; rival is hidden by paper.
00:03-00:05: paper opens; rival rises dusted with confetti, giving a confused thumbs-up.
00:05-00:06: Captain says, “A unanimous-ish result!”; return to results.

2. Baron Boardroom — Quarterly Reckoning
00:00-00:01: invoice close-up; Baron says, “This will be itemized.”
00:01-00:03: paper hands stamp abstract symbols and fold a cardboard crate.
00:03-00:05: crate bursts into packing confetti; rival sits on top wearing a shipping label.
00:05-00:06: Baron says, “Expenses approved.”; restore results.

3. Doctor Broadcast — Sponsored Break
00:00-00:01: ring light frames the action; Doctor says, “A brief wellness interruption!”
00:01-00:03: rival flattens into a generic cardboard product cutout.
00:03-00:05: floating fake-review stars bounce; cutout unfolds into rival with stickers.
00:05-00:06: Doctor says, “Ask your imagination if it is right for you.”; restore results.

4. General Gravy — Recipe Revision
00:00-00:01: recipe card fills frame; General says, “Time for a softer serving!”
00:01-00:03: flour poof turns rival into a plump clay dumpling.
00:03-00:05: bell rings and confetti herbs fall; dumpling unfolds back to rival.
00:05-00:06: General says, “Perfectly underwhelming!”; restore results.

5. General Gadget — Autonomous Delivery
00:00-00:01: drones project a generic route; Gadget says, “Automation has entered the chat!”
00:01-00:03: wobbly cardboard delivery cart loses a wheel and gently bumps rival.
00:03-00:05: foam packing peanuts and panels pop; rival emerges wearing a box hat.
00:05-00:06: Gadget says, “Version one point oh-ish!”; restore results.

6. Influence Oracle — Terms Accepted
00:00-00:01: abstract phone UI frames rival; Oracle says, “Engagement is mandatory!”
00:01-00:03: rival safely becomes a generic SKIP button.
00:03-00:05: Oracle taps the button; soft boings and reaction bubbles appear; rival pops out with notification stickers.
00:05-00:06: Oracle says, “Remember to un-follow gravity!”; restore results.

For each, create a 6-10 panel storyboard, timeline YAML, source animation list, camera cut list, subtitle file, event/cue sheet, VFX and audio inventory, cleanup test, and provenance/originality records.
```
