# Arena, HUD, and Arcade Overlay Prompt

```text
Create two wholly original arenas and UI assets.

Arena A, “Municipal Parade Depot”: a theatrical warehouse containing blank parade floats, cardboard city façades, folded banners with invented symbols, and safe foreground ropes. Use 5-7 parallax layers, warm/cool stage lighting, and strong dark midground contrast so fighters remain readable. No government seals, flags, party marks, or real landmarks.

Arena B, “Wellness Mall Studio”: a fictional mall atrium with a generic broadcast booth, empty store signs, foam plants, and translucent plastic skylights. Use parallax layers but no gameplay collision or hazards in the initial prototype.

Create HUD assets: high-contrast molded-plastic health bars, meter, round pips, portraits, pause panel, results panel, character select grid, stage select cards, and training overlay. Make it readable at 1280x720 and 1920x1080; support reduced screen shake and color/contrast alternatives.

Create an original arcade-button overlay. Show a joystick/D-pad and two 3x2 button banks labeled LIGHT/MEDIUM/HEAVY and SPECIAL/THROW/START. Provide pressed, released, disabled, remappable, and high-contrast states. Use generic colored plastic buttons; do not reproduce any cabinet, logo, or commercial layout artwork.
```
